# GIVTA PLATFORM RULES & GUIDELINES

## 🎯 **GIVTA CHALLENGE PLATFORM OVERVIEW**

Givta is Nigeria's premier social tipping platform that combines fitness, personal development, competitions, and community building through challenge-based tipping. Users create challenges, join competitions, and tip participants while building positive societal impact.

### 📋 **PLATFORM OBJECTIVES**
- Encourage personal growth and community motivation
- Create transparent fundraising and reward systems
- Build trust through verified transactions and user accountability
- Foster positive social interactions and healthy competition
- Support brand partnerships for social impact initiatives

---

## 🏆 **GENERAL PLATFORM RULES**

### 🤝 **USER CONDUCT & ETHICS**
1. **Honesty & Authenticity**: All achievements and progress must be genuine
2. **Respectful Communication**: No harassment, bullying, or offensive language
3. **Privacy Respect**: Do not share other users' personal information without consent
4. **Fraud Prevention**: No manipulation of tip amounts or challenge outcomes
5. **Platform Abuse**: No bot usage, multiple accounts, or system exploitation

### 💰 **TIPPING & FINANCIAL RULES**
1. **Transaction Security**: All payments processed through verified Paystack gateway
2. **Platform Fees**: 5% platform fee on all tipping transactions
3. **No Refunds**: Tipping transactions are final and non-refundable
4. **Minimum Tips**: ₦50 minimum tip amount per transaction
5. **Winner Payouts**: Automatic payouts within 24-48 hours of challenge completion

### ⚡ **PLATFORM FEATURES**
1. **Wallet System**: Secure internal wallet for tip storage and withdrawals
2. **Real-time Updates**: Live leaderboards and progress tracking
3. **Push Notifications**: WhatsApp and app notifications for challenge updates
4. **Shareable Content**: Social media integration for challenge promotion
5. **Customer Support**: 24/7 support through WhatsApp and in-app chat

## 🎮 **CHALLENGE TYPES SPECIFIC RULES**

## 🎁 **PERSONAL CHALLENGES**

### **How It Works:**
1. **Challenge Creator** sets personal goals and target tip amounts
2. **Friends & Family** tip the creator as motivation throughout the challenge
3. **Progress Updates** shared via photos, videos, or text
4. **Bonus Rewards**: 2-10% bonus earned based on goal completion percentage

### **Participation Rules:**
- ✅ Goal amounts must be reasonable and achievable
- ✅ Progress updates must show genuine effort
- ✅ Media proof required for fitness/health challenges
- ✅ Weekly progress sharing mandatory for challenges >7 days

### **Tipping Guidelines:**
- 💰 Tip anytime during the challenge period
- 💰 Multiple tips allowed from same user
- 💰 Tips are distributed: 95% to creator, 5% platform fee
- 💰 Bonus pool: Additional 10% added based on completion rate

### **Violation Consequences:**
- 🚫 False progress updates → Challenge suspension
- 🚫 Goal manipulation → Account warning/to ban
- 🚫 Spam tipping → Tip limits applied

---

## 🏆 **COMPETITION CHALLENGES**

### **How It Works:**
1. **Entry Fee Competition**: Participants pay entry fee to join
2. **Live Tipping**: Supporters tip competitors during challenge period
3. **Prize Pool**: Entry fees + tips create competition prize pool
4. **Winner Distribution**: 60% First place, 30% Second, 10% Third

### **Entry & Participation:**
- 💵 Entry fees range from ₦500 - ₦50,000
- 💵 Minimum participants to start: 3 users
- 💵 Maximum participants: 100 per challenge
- 💵 Entry fee goes toprize pool + platform revenue
- 💵 Refund policy: No refunds once competition starts

### **Tipping Dynamics:**
- 💰 Any user (participants/supporters) can tip competitors
- 💰 Real-time leaderboard updates based on total tips received
- 💰 Participants can tip each other (self-promotion encouraged)
- 💰 Anonymous tipping available for privacy

### **Winner Determination:**
- 🏅 Highest total tip amount wins 1st place (60% of prize pool)
- 🏅 Second highest wins 2nd place (30% of prize pool)
- 🏅 Third highest wins 3rd place (10% of prize pool)
- 🏅 Ties resolved by: First registered participant wins

### **Prize Distribution:**
- 💰 Automatic payout within 24 hours of challenge end
- 💰 Payouts delivered to verified wallets/banks
- 💰 Tax implications: Users responsible for own tax reporting
- 💰 Platform cut: 5% of all tipping transactions

### **Competition Rules:**
- ⚔️ Competitions must be skill-based or goal-oriented
- ⚔️ No manipulation of tip amounts between participants
- ⚔️ Fair play required - violation leads to disqualification

## 🚫 **PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES**

### **Content & Behavior:**
1. **Illegal Content**: Gambling, drugs, violence, adult content
2. **Hate Speech**: Discrimination by race, gender, religion, etc.
3. **Harassment**: Bullying, threats, intimidation
4. **Spam**: Unsolicited promotions, repetitive posting
5. **Impersonation**: Fake identities or brand misrepresentation

### **Challenge Categories:**
1. **Prohibited Types**: Any challenge promoting illegal activities
2. **Health Claims**: No false medical or health claims
3. **Financial Scams**: No pyramid schemes or financial fraud
4. **Underage Content**: All participants must be 18+ years old

### **Technical Violations:**
1. **System Abuse**: Bots, automation, multiple accounts
2. **Payment Manipulation**: Bypassing official payment channels
3. **Data Mining**: Scraping user data without authorization
4. **Security Breaches**: Attempting to hack or compromise systems
